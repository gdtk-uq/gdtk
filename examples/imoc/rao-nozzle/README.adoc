= Rao thrust-optimised nozzle
`gdtk/examples/imoc/rao-nozzle`

Jeremy Moran,
2026-09-15

This example designs a thrust-optimised axisymmetric (bell) nozzle with the
`gdtk.imoc` method-of-characteristics package, following Rao's method as
implemented by J. Kunze in the original Tcl IMOC code and described in
section 3.1 of his PhD thesis.  A centred expansion fan at the wall point A
is grown until the mass flow crossing the last fan characteristic AD
balances the mass flow crossing the control surface DE, along which the two
Lagrange-multiplier expressions of the variational problem hold their
nozzle-lip values.  The wall contour is then traced as the streamline
running from A to the nozzle lip E.

The test case is nozzle A of Rao (1958): M_A=1.103 on the (straight)
initial characteristic, lip Mach number M_E=3.5, gamma=1.23, vacuum
back-pressure, and a throat-arc radius R_A/r_A=0.45.  The computed contour,
wall Mach number and wall angle are compared with data digitised from
Fig 3.3 of the thesis, which overlays Rao's original results and Kunze's
IMOC solution.
//end::description[]

== Running

  $ ./run.sh

The design itself takes about a second and writes `nozzle-wall.data`
(columns: x/r_A, r/r_A, Mach, theta in degrees) along with a summary of the
run, including the mass-flow balance and RMS deviations from the digitised
reference data.  The gnuplot scripts produce `rao-nozzle-contour.eps`,
`rao-nozzle-mach.eps` and `rao-nozzle-theta.eps`.

== Notes

- The centred-corner approximation is used at A: the initial characteristic
  is a straight line carrying uniform axial flow at M_A, and the full
  expansion happens at the point A.  For presentation in the same form as
  Rao's figure, the corner is unrolled onto the circular throat arc of
  radius R_A (on which the wall turns from 0 to theta_max carrying the
  corner-fan flow values) and the computed wall attaches at the arc end.
- Computed nozzle: theta_max = 32.7 deg, length x_E/r_A = 8.28 and exit
  radius r_E/r_A = 4.41, against approximately 34.5 deg, 8.19 and 4.39
  digitised from Rao's data.  The wall Mach number and angle at the lip
  reproduce M_E and theta_E to better than 0.01.  The residual difference
  in theta_max is inherited from the straight-initial-characteristic
  approximation and from digitising a steeply-rising curve near the throat.
- The mass-flow balance converges to a relative error below 1e-6 and the
  traced wall closes on the lip E to within 0.006 r_A.
